**Batching** (the 0.4.0 and 0.5.0 retros: units that touch the same functions wait for each other):

- Wave 1 in separate code: own-rate-2 (the merge, lane times, drag and drop), trig-2 (fvb-core's signal maths, the
  formula evaluator), docking (the docking module's resize, captions, tab strips).
- focus waits for docking (both change the docking module's presses and focus); view waits for own-rate-2 (both change
  the lanes); encodings, records and scaling start as slots free.
- At most three implementers and three clones at once; implementers on the standard model; reviews, the final review
  and fix rounds 4–5 on the most capable. Every dispatch and resume watched (`tools/watch-report.sh`); each unit's CI
  read before every review.
