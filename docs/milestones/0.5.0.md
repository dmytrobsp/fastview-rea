# Milestone 0.5.0

## Plan

**Why these go together** (chosen by the maintainer, 2026-10-30, from the controller's candidates A + B):

- **A — calculation fidelity:** values ours prints that can differ from the original's in the last digits or in
  whether they are computed at all: the original's sin / cos bit for bit (#72), the display factor applied per sample
  before the DFT, phasor, RMS and a0 (#60), own rates after a merge and the rate test of «Совместить каналы» (#57,
  #71), the ОМП flags after «Кодировка» (#66), a kV channel's unit in the stats rows (#86).
- **B — panes:** the auto-hide strip's placement, its per-tab buttons, the slid-out pane's divider and the ribbon's
  toggle on an auto-hidden pane (#61–#64); a floating pane no longer acting as a modal dialog (#70); the focus leaving
  a closed pane's grid (#77); a short drag to float a pane, with a move cursor (#90).

**Batching** (the 0.4.0 retro: when two units touch the same functions, the second waits for the first to land):

- Wave 1 at once, in separate code: trig (fvb-core's signal maths and the sample readers), autohide (the docking
  module's auto-hide strip and slide-out), small (the ОМП flags, the stats rows' units).
- own-rate waits for trig: both change how a channel's samples and period reach the phasor / RMS / spectrum code.
- panes waits for autohide: both change the docking module (drag, focus, the modal test).
- At most three implementers and three Windows clones at once. Every implementer runs on the standard model (each
  unit reads the original and compares in a clone); reviews, the final review and fix rounds 4–5 on the most capable.

**The maintainer's decisions** (2026-10-30):

- #60: the display factor is applied per sample before the DFT, phasor, RMS and a0, as the original does. Improvement:
  the fundamental's label always reads «100» in «% of the fundamental» mode (the original's «100.0» is a rounding
  accident of its "%2.1f below 100" branch) — into IMPROVEMENTS.md with the change.
- #72: port Intel's BSD-3-Clause SSE2 sin / cos (bionic's x86 libm), with ties to even, as the one sin / cos of
  fvb-core's signal maths (spectrum, phasors, harmonic RMS, frequency correction); the spike matched msvcr120 bit for
  bit below 90 112. Intel's copyright notice stays in the source's header; `LICENSE-intel-libm.txt` in the repository
  root and in the release archives, and a line in the README's licences. Also match the original's angle order,
  j·(k·2π)/w. Values beyond 90 112 and non-finite ones fall back to std.
- #90: a docked pane floats once the caption drag has moved Windows' drag distance (4 px at 100 %, scaled with the
  display) from the press, wherever the pointer is; the four-way move cursor from the press on the caption and while
  dragging; the caption's buttons never start a drag. An improvement where the original differs (IMPROVEMENTS.md).
- Not in this milestone: #93 (a flat channel's harmonics as «0») waits for the user's answer.
