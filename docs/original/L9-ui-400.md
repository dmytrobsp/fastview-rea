# L9.400–L9.499 — UI layer entries

Part of [L9 — UI layer](L9-ui.md); index in [README](README.md).

### L9.400 — VM (a clone): «Стиль» and Help pressed while their menu is open  ·  ✅
With «Стиль»'s menu open the original's button keeps the Office 2007
small button's checked face ((167, 142, 102) top, the two shaded rows,
the gold face and sides), under the pointer the small hot face; its
face spans x 1196–1246, rows 30–51 at 1280 wide. Ours drew a generic
pale box for both, 49 px wide and a row low. Now `tab_row_hot` paints
`checked_bg` while the button's menu is open and `hot_small` under the
pointer (the older looks keep their box), on whole pixels from row 30;
«Стиль» is 51 px with its label and arrow where they were. VM: the face's
edges at 1196 / 1246 and its column within a few units.
Also seen at 1280 × 800 (no change): the ruler's right-click menu in
the difference band as ours; ours' first-run frame is 945 × 536 shown
against the original's 943 × 533, so Частотная docks a pixel taller
(145 against 144; residual).

### L9.401 — VM (a clone): a dialog taller than the work area hangs from its top  ·  ✅
«Отображение каналов» (DIALOG 463, a 356 × 752 px client) does not fit
the 752 px work area of a 1280 × 800 screen: the original shows it from
row 0, caption and all, running on under the taskbar; ours centred it
on the frame, its caption 13 px above the screen. MFC's CenterWindow
ends by moving the window rect into the monitor's work area — its right
and bottom edges first, then its left and top — which ours left out.
Now `dialogs::work_area_fit` does that step for every dialog, ours'
window standing for the work area (maximised, it is). VM: the caption
from row 0, the list from 26, the sides at 461 / 818 in both. Test
`a_tall_dialog_keeps_its_caption_on_the_screen`.

### L9.402 — VM (a clone): the application menu's lower items have images  ·  ✅
Side by side at 1280 × 800 the application menu matches but for its
images: the original has one on every item (its bitmaps, W6), ours left
«Проводник», «Комментарии», «Загрузка настроек...», «Настройки» and
«Закрыть» blank. Now ours draws its own for them (`icons::Icon::{Explorer,
Comments, LoadSettings, Settings, CloseDoc}`: a page under a magnifier,
a note, a folder under a list, a gear, a folder with a cross); greyed
with their item. Also compared (no change): «Коэффициенты» and «Параметры
ОМП» sit and lay out as the original's on the small screen.

### L9.403 — VM (a clone): the differential window's first push runs on a plane too small to paint  ·  ✅
After a first run at the default frame and a maximise, the original's
differential plane («Нет») has its origin pushed left only, the x axis
through the middle (row 581); ours had pushed it down to the bottom too.
Read by hand: the painter `0x445e00` tests the client against 120
(`0x445eaf` / `0x445ebe`) and on a small one jumps to `0x44611f`, its
tail — which still runs the one-shot push to the lower left
(`0x44612d`–`0x44619e`, limits `0x443620` from the client then). At the
first paint the plane is a few dozen pixels tall, so y has no room and
only x moves. Ours returned before the push and pushed later, on the
maximised plane. Now the small-plane return does the push first. VM:
the x axis on row 581 in both; the pane itself a pixel left (L9.394's
maximised residual). Test
`the_differential_push_runs_on_a_small_first_paint`.
