### L9.405 — An auto-hidden pane stays out while the pointer rests on its strip button or the pane holds the focus  ·  ✅ (#4, PR #9)

Read (mfc120u, by hand): the auto-hide check that the 700 ms timer runs (`0x10087a4a`) leaves the pane out in any of these cases:
- the pane's active flag (+0x2c8) is set. It is refreshed at each idle update: set when the focus window is the pane or a child of it (`0x10089d7d`, GetFocus / IsChild; a change repaints the caption). It is also set when the pane is shown with activation (`0x10089800`).
  The focus comes from three sources:
  - a press on a strip button: the strip's WM_LBUTTONDOWN (`0x1005f612`) sets the focus to the button's pane;
  - a press on the pane's caption or its buttons: WM_LBUTTONDOWN (`0x10086d23`) gives it to the pane, or to its one child window, and WM_RBUTTONDOWN (`0x10086f01`) to the pane;
  - a press in a window inside the pane.
  The focus leaves a pane that hides: Windows hands a hidden window's focus to its parent;
- a second flag (+0x30c) is set. Only an exported setter (ordinal 13774) writes it, and neither mfc120u nor FastView calls that setter, so it stays 0;
- the pane is not in auto-hide mode;
- a popup menu is up;
- the pane's tooltip (+0x338, created as a dock-bar tooltip) is visible;
- the window under the cursor is the pane, its divider (+0x33c) or a property-grid tooltip (runtime class `0x10018988`). It finds that window with WindowFromPoint and walks up through its parents;
- the cursor (GetCursorPos) is in the pane's window rect joined with its divider's rect;
- the cursor is in the pane's auto-hide button rect, mapped from its strip to the screen.

Only when none of these holds does the check slide the pane back. Opening on hover comes from the strip's own mouse moves (timer 0xec00, 400 ms; L9.92).

VM (original, WIN_1999), with the Векторная group pinned:
- Hovering the strip button slid the pane out. With the pointer moved over the view, it was back within 1.5 s.
- After a click in the vector diagram, with the pointer moved away, the pane was still out 4.5 s later.
- After a click on the strip button, with the pointer over the view and no other click, it was still out 4.5 s later.
- In both of the last two cases, a click in the oscillogram view slid it back.
- After a strip click, → left В1 where the strip click put it; after a click in the view, → moved it one small step on.
- Copy after a strip click, after a click in a diagram, and after a press on the Векторная, Дифференциальная or Частотная caption gave a metafile and no document. With the view focused, Copy gave a metafile and the document.

Ours drew the slid-out pane in an egui area sized by its content. A pane part-way out lays out wider than its rect, and the area, kept on screen, moved left as it grew until it covered the strip. The strip button lost its hover, so the check closed the pane and the hover reopened it, over and over. The slide never rested at fully out, so the pane's edge shook and the stale-size click rect covered the strip on the right and bottom edges. Ours also ignored the focus and repainted every 16 ms while a pane was out.

Now:
- The slide rests at its target.
- The area is exactly the pane's rect, on a pixel, with no fade.
- The area claims its click rect again with this frame's rect.
- The check counts the pointer as inside when it is in the pane's rect or on one of the group's buttons, by position.
- The focus belongs to the pressed window's group. Each of these gives it:
  - a press in a diagram or grid (the copy focus, L9.58), resolved topmost-first, with a slid-out pane taking presses over what it covers;
  - a press on a caption;
  - a press on a strip button.
- A slid-out group holding the focus stays out until the focus goes elsewhere.
- The focus returns to the view when its group hides.
- A group hidden while out comes back in its strip.
- Frames are requested only for the slide and the two timers.

A fully slid-out pane renders pixel-identical to before (`FV_WINDOW_STATE=autohide`). VM (ours): the diagram-click and strip-click sequences give the same results as the original. That includes → leaving В1 in place after a strip click, and Copy giving a metafile with no document.

### L9.406 — One point is one device pixel at any display scale  ·  ✅ (#5, #6, PR #11)

The original is system-DPI aware (manifest `dpiAware`). On the primary monitor at the sign-in scale, Windows
does not stretch it, and its views paint in device pixels with fonts of fixed cell heights (Tahoma 15 / 13,
`0x404df9`) and 1 px pens. On a clone at 125 % and 150 %, its lanes, waveforms, rulers and diagrams are pixel

egui's own Ctrl-zoom is off. Improvement over the original (docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md): on a monitor at another
scale, or after a live scale change, ours stays 1:1 and crisp where Windows bitmap-stretches the original. The
chrome's size above 100 %: #12 (PR #14). Tests `fractional_display_scales_paint_device_pixels`,

### L9.407 — A COMTRADE 2013 UTF-8 export: the `.cfg` read as UTF-8, «Кодировка» on it, the ADC code with the unit multiplier, one compute at open  ·  ✅ (#8, PR #10)

**The original.**
- It widens every `.cfg` token through `Display Encoding` (`0x422300` → `0x42cbb0`, default 1251) and keeps it as an ANSI string. It knows no BOM: a C37.111-2013 export in UTF-8 shows mojibake and «п»ї» before the station under the default, and the right names with «?» for the BOM and U+FFFD under UTF-8 (VM).
- It reads the 2013 `time_code,local_code` line for revision "2013" only and never reads `tmq_code,leapsec` (`0x450e70`).
- ASCII samples are `m·a·x + b` in doubles (`0x451800`). The cfg min / max are integers that are read and copied but never used (`0x422460`).
- The ADC code is `(v − b)/(m·a)`, rounded by `0x411bf0`, then truncated to int32 (`0x422250`).
- The `.hdr` is kept and written back but never shown (`0x4492e0`); its guess is stored as `Comments/Codepage` (`0x450d1f`).
- The record code page is OEM / ANSI (`doc+0x6e8`, `0x44a112`, `0x44a330` → `0x4543e0`). ANSI is the names as read; OEM is their ANSI bytes read as 866 (VM). It is guessed at every open (`0x459e60` at `0x44f7eb`), and a stored `Codepage` wins (`0x44f808`).
- It recomputes on the `.set` apply (`0x44f5b0`).
- The large record opens in ≈ 20–30 s at ≈ 990 MB.

**Ours before.** It produced the same mojibake. On a UTF-8 / KOI8-R record, «Кодировка» offered only 866 / 1251 (shown as «866 MS-DOS», no way back) and ignored the stored `Codepage`. It divided ADC codes by `a` alone, with Rust's rounding, and returned the value itself for |a| ≤ 1e‑12. It computed an opened record twice.

**Ours now.**
- A `.cfg` / `.cff` with a UTF-8 BOM, or of revision 2013 and plausible multi-byte UTF-8, is read as UTF-8 whatever the setting (BOM stripped, an exporter's U+FFFD kept; `Oscillogram::text_enc` / `text_bom`).
- Such a record is saved all in UTF-8, with a BOM as needed. Its valid-UTF-8 `.hdr` is read as UTF-8 and saved byte for byte.
- «Кодировка»'s 866 / 1251 on it are the original's OEM / ANSI over the names as read, and a third entry, its own encoding, gives them back losslessly (`Doc::read_enc`, `codepage_items`, `decode_name` / `name_bytes_of` in `Doc::set_codepage`; a stored `Codepage` ≠ 0 selects it); #13's references follow.
- `AnalogChannel::adc_code` divides by `m·a` with `0x411bf0`'s rounding and keeps codes beyond ±2³¹ whole.
- At an open, the `.set` apply skips its recompute when nothing it reads changed.
- The divergences are in `docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md`.
- Ours opens the sample in 2.9 s / 606 MB (reader alone 0.44 s / 283 MB).

**Tests:** `comtrade_2013` (synthetic record, including the save round trip), `a_utf8_record_keeps_utf8_names_and_hdr`, `a_utf8_record_s_references_follow_the_code_page`, `a_utf8_record_s_alias_survives_the_first_switch`, `a_stored_code_page_applies_to_a_utf8_record`, `an_open_recomputes_only_when_the_settings_change_the_values`.

**Residual / as the original (no change):** the 2013 `time_code,local_code` line is not written back on save, and the writer's analog min / max leave the unit multiplier in (`(v − b)/a`) — both as the original's writer (`0x44dfa0`, `0x425530`).

Improvements over the original (docs/IMPROVEMENTS.md): see PR #10. Residual: the KOI8-R limit on і ї є ґ in pasted / merged names and a BOM inside a `.cff` HDR section (deferred to the milestone's final review).

### L9.409 — The ОМП's «КЗ фазы» flags keep their names across «Кодировка» and are found by name  ·  ✅ (#66, PR #94)

The ОМП's «КЗ фазы А / B / C» flags are computed channels (bit 13, L9.188). «Кодировка» (`0x4543e0` → `0x425960`)
shows such a channel's name as stored (the raw name, `+0x64`, into the shown name, `+0x68`) while the record's own
channels are re-decoded, so the flags' names do not change across a switch. The clear (`0x4a17a0`) deletes the
result channel and the three flags through `0x42ecf0`, which compares each channel's shown name (`0x401bd0`) with
the string-table names (ids 0xbf2, 0xc99, 0xc97, 0xc98); the run (`0x42ead0` → `0x42edf0`) likewise finds existing
flags by name, so a record's own digital channel with a flag's name is overwritten by the run and deleted by the
clear. Ours re-decoded the flags and then looked for them by their literal names: after 1251 → 866 the clear left
garbled flags and a re-run added three more. Now the flags carry a marker kept in step with the channels; the run
looks each phase up by its own name (a marked flag first, then any channel of that name), the clear removes marked
flags and channels of a flag's name, the names are kept across a switch and stored with an empty alias. Checked by
reading the binary (no clone run of ОМП). Tests `code_page_switch_keeps_the_flags_findable`,
`flags_are_stored_without_an_alias_after_a_code_page_switch`, `an_own_channel_named_like_a_flag_is_taken_by_name`,
`a_rerun_after_a_take_over_keeps_the_phases_apart`.

A kV / kA channel's unit in the stats rows (#86) is not a difference: on a clone, on synthetic kV / kA records, the
original and ours print the same text in the value column and in the СКЗ / A1 rows — the SI prefix follows each
number's magnitude (`0x43d960`, the text through `0x42bc50`, L9.74–L9.76), so one kV channel reads «кВ» at 100 kV and
«В» at a few hundred volts in both. Test `kilovolt_and_kiloampere_units_in_every_row` pins every row's text.
